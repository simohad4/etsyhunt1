# M4.6 offline execution safety assessment

`researchExecutionSafety.ts` contains pure deterministic assessments, not enforcement. All evidence is synthetic/unverified, including caller-supplied claims of authoritative or fresh evidence. Every output is deeply frozen, reports only HOLD or REVIEW, and keeps activationBlocked=true, executionAuthorized=false, automaticRetryAllowed=false and requiresAuthoritativeDatabaseValidation=true. REVIEW means inspect evidence, never dispatch. No route, provider, database, clock, environment, credential, retry or billing executor is used. Identifiers are validated for comparisons but not echoed in results. Fixed reason/control codes contain no raw errors or secrets.

## Functions and input contract

Every input is an exact-key plain data record; required fields must be supplied, with explicit null for unknown nullable values. Accessors, callbacks, unexpected fields, unsafe integers and invalid enums reject to a fixed HOLD assessment. Standard validation is not a sandbox for arbitrary hostile JavaScript proxies. No user payload or callback is invoked. Components are independent evidence exercises, not linked records of an authenticated job.

- `assessResearchCost(scenarioInput)` delegates entirely to M4.4's provisional calculator: pages/listings, explicit token scenarios and independent one/two total attempts. It retains incomplete-cost flags and null unknown charges. `UNKNOWN_COSTS` and `CAP_EXCEEDED` always force HOLD, including when the known included-cost subtotal fits the cap. Active prices are neither changed nor bypassed; actual billing is not established.
- `assessDispatchOwnership({workerId,claimWorkerId,durableClaim,claimState,contendingWorkers,fence,currentFence,submissionIntent,provider,providerIdentifier,providerRequestId})`: nullable IDs/fences, claimState missing/current/stale/expired, intent missing/recorded/uncertain, provider apify/openrouter. Pin providerIdentifier to the existing actor/model. Exactly one contender, matching owner and positive current fencing values are necessary for REVIEW, never sufficient for real ownership. Missing/stale/conflicting evidence HOLD. Missing provider identity or uncertain submission HOLD; expired leases never authorize resubmission. Provider IDs may legitimately be absent before a send, but this conservative assessment still holds that boundary for separate design rather than pretending it is dispatched safely.
- `assessResearchOutcome({requestId,attemptId,unresolvedOutcome,outcome,providerRequestId,reconciliation})`: outcome is not_submitted/accepted/completed/definitive_failure/unknown/timeout/disconnected/partial. The explicit unresolved latch is monotonic across stale snapshots unless matching reconciliation evidence is supplied. Reconciliation is null or `{requestId,attemptId,providerRequestId,authoritative,fresh,decision}`, with decision not_submitted/completed/definitive_failure. Matching IDs, freshness/authority claims and consistent decisions are required for advisory resolution. Submitted outcomes without provider IDs HOLD. The result exposes advisoryUnresolvedOutcome and reconciliationAssessment, but authoritativeResolutionVerified always remains false. A completed claim is not permission to settle, publish or retry. The caller must retain the latch across invocations; this stateless module cannot stop a caller from discarding history.
- `assessResearchBilling({estimatedMicroUsd,reportedMicroUsd,invoicedMicroUsd,invoiceAuthoritative,recordsConflict,settledUsingEstimate,lateChargeMicroUsd})`: amounts are null or nonnegative safe integers. Unknown charges remain null. Explicit zero is distinguishable from missing. A reported/invoiced difference emits `BILLING_RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED` and HOLD, not an automatic conflict finding: differences may involve adjustments or billing scope requiring evidence review. `BILLING_CONFLICT` is reserved for explicitly supplied `recordsConflict:true` evidence; that assertion itself remains synthetic, not an externally confirmed contradiction. Both reasons may coexist. Estimated-only settlement, positive late charges or absent invoice authority also HOLD. Even matching invoice evidence is only invoice_reconciled_claim; finalCostsVerified always remains false because evidence is synthetic and no invoice is authenticated. Estimates can differ from actual costs without automatically implying a conflict. No totals pretend to resolve the meaning of a late adjustment; overflow of supplied invoice plus late liability is rejected. Real accounting must establish whether an invoice includes an adjustment to avoid double counting.
- `assessProposedTrial({identityVerified,priorGrants,concurrentRedemptions,proposedGlobalBudgetMicroUsd,reservedMicroUsd,settledMicroUsd,unresolvedLiabilityMicroUsd})`: evaluates a proposed one-credit/36-listing trial at the existing two-attempt reserve scenario. Identity/grant/redemption evidence is synthetic only. Any existing grant, concurrent redemption, positive/unknown liability or insufficient proposal budget is flagged. Known committed amounts are summed with BigInt; null liability is excluded from that known subtotal but always blocks any budget-fit claim, never treated as known zero. The operational budget is always zero; trialGranted/budgetReserved stay false. No subscription is changed. The current schema lacks the entitlement even when all evidence appears favorable.
- `assessResearchExecutionSafety({observation,cost,ownership,outcome,billing,trial})`: revalidates the observation through M4.1 and includes each assessment and its reason codes. It always HOLDs, preserving exact planner classification/status/review. The observation cannot clear the independently supplied unresolved-outcome latch. No fixture or planner snapshot proves ownership, payment or execution admission.

All results include stable reasonCodes, evidenceGaps and nextRequiredControls. Gaps are reason codes requiring evidence/control review; they are not authenticated findings about live state. Malformed inputs produce INVALID_EVIDENCE and monetary overflow ARITHMETIC_OVERFLOW, without echoing supplied values. The object fields remain bounded, there are no event histories, and amounts use existing safe micro-USD semantics.

## Authoritative persistence still required

Advisory reconciliation compares synthetic claims only. Caller-provided `authoritative:true` and `fresh:true` are not externally verified. A `resolution_candidate` can change the returned advisory latch when identities/decisions match, but never writes or clears a durable database latch, settles an attempt, or permits a retry. Without matching reconciliation, a previously supplied unresolved latch survives stale or completed snapshots. The caller still owns persistence; these functions cannot prevent the caller from discarding prior evidence.

Authoritative database reconciliation is a separate future operation: authenticate and bind external evidence to the exact request/attempt/provider identity, validate freshness and conflicting records, then perform an audited atomic transition through a separately reviewed SQL/RPC contract. Billing finality additionally requires authoritative external billing verification and late-adjustment handling. None of those operations exists in this offline assessment.

M2 already records reservations, attempts, external IDs, usage and estimated/reported costs, but does not implement durable worker ownership, send intent/fencing/leases, late billing adjustments or trial grants. Its one-active-attempt index prevents multiple admitted rows, not duplicate external sends. Replayed begin responses cannot grant dispatch. A future separately reviewed SQL/RPC design must bind claims to owner/job/attempt/request digest; fence stale workers; persist submission intent and uncertain sends; retain provider run/request identities; and prohibit takeover resubmission after an unknown outcome. Provider idempotency must not be assumed.

Add authoritative audited reconciliation for held jobs and active/finished attempts, including late invoice events, unique billing evidence identities, append-only adjustments and global/account liability controls. Current finished outcomes are immutable and estimated fallback accounting is not verified billing. Missing usage must not release cost as zero. Completion/publication and refunds must remain tied to exact attempts and the original credit generation. No automatic paid retry is authorized by any failure category, expired lease or REVIEW result.

Trial entitlements require atomic one-time grants/redemptions, trusted identity eligibility, deletion/recreation and linked-account policy, separate trial budget reservations, concurrency/rate controls, unresolved-liability holds and a kill switch. A one-credit balance alone does not meet M2's paid-subscription admission rules. No operational trial budget is enabled here.

Before paid integration: verify actor build/full output/completion contracts and routing/schema capabilities; obtain verified applicable prices and surcharge/failure terms; resolve the existing cap/economics mismatch without simply raising a cap; implement durable dispatch/reconciliation/entitlements; separately verify staging parity and concurrency. Offline tests and prior rollback evidence do not certify real provider billing or production readiness.
